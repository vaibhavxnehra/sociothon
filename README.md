## PROJECT BRIEF

Build a stunning, highly animated, modern single-page (with smooth-scroll sections + a few sub-pages) website to promote and drive registrations for **"SOCI-O-THON & NIRMAAN — Together for Social Good,"** a dual event under **SOUHARDYA 2026**, hosted by **Rajiv Gandhi Institute of Petroleum Technology (RGIPT), Jais, Amethi** — an Institute of National Importance under the Government of India. The audience is undergraduate students from colleges across India, so the site must feel premium, credible, exciting, and easy to register through, on both desktop and mobile.

Design direction: **dynamic, cinematic, motion-rich** — scroll-triggered reveals, parallax, glassmorphism/gradient panels on a deep blue theme (matching the brochure's navy/gold palette), animated counters, hover micro-interactions, and a strong sense of "this is a big, national-level event." Avoid generic template look — this should feel bespoke, energetic, and trustworthy at once (government-affiliated institute + youth hackathon energy).

---

## 1. OVERALL VIBE & VISUAL SYSTEM

- **Color palette:** Deep navy/royal blue (#0B2A4A–#123A63 range) as the primary background, gold/amber (#D4A017–#F2C94C) as the accent for CTAs and highlights, white for text/cards, with soft gradient meshes and subtle globe/world-map textures in the background (echoing the brochure's world-map motif).
- **Typography:** Bold, modern display font for headings (something like Poppins/Sora/Clash Display), clean sans-serif for body (Inter/Manto). Strong hierarchy, generous whitespace.
- **Motion:** Use Framer Motion (or equivalent) throughout:
  - Hero section: animated gradient background, floating/parallax icons (globe, hands holding globe, lightbulb, puzzle pieces — echoing brochure imagery), animated headline text (staggered fade/slide-in), a subtle particle or constellation effect.
  - Scroll-triggered fade/slide-up animations for every section as it enters viewport.
  - Animated number counters for prize pool (₹30,000), dates, sponsorship tiers, etc.
  - Hover effects on all cards (lift + glow + scale), buttons (magnetic/ripple effect), and nav links (underline draw animation).
  - A sticky/animated countdown timer to key deadlines (Registration opens, Round 1 deadline, Final Round).
  - Smooth scroll navigation with an active-section-highlighting sticky navbar.
  - Page transitions should feel fluid, not jarring.
- **Responsiveness:** Fully responsive, mobile-first polish — this will be shared heavily on WhatsApp/Instagram, so mobile experience must be flawless.

### D. SOCI-O-THON — Social Innovation Hackathon
- Description: "Together for Social Good" — the first hackathon of its kind at this scale, open to students from every field (not just engineering), tackling grassroots-to-citywide social problems.
- **Full Rulebook / Process (as a 4-step animated vertical timeline/stepper with icons):**
  1. **Idea Proposal (Round 1):** Submit a short write-up covering problem understanding, proposed solution, impact potential, and feasibility (PPT/PDF, online submission).
  2. **Shortlisting:** Teams shortlisted from Round 1 entries and invited to the Final Round.
  3. **Prototype Development:** Selected teams develop a prototype/model, implementation strategy, impact assessment, and sustainability plan.
  4. **Final Evaluation:** Live presentation of the prototype on Final Round day, followed by a Q&A round with judges who decide the winners.
- **Themes** (display as 5 animated icon cards): Rural Development · Health & Hygiene · Energy & Sustainability · Women & Child Empowerment · Mental Wellbeing & Community Care.
- **Prize Pool:** ₹30,000 (Special/Additional Awards and Recognitions) — show as a large animated counter/badge.
- Certificate of Participation awarded to every participant.

### E. NIRMAAN — National Social Conference
- Description: organized under SOUHARDYA, The Social Fest — a conference and presentation competition bringing students face-to-face with IAS/IPS officers, senior government officials, and NGO representatives working at the grassroots of social change. Includes interactive talks, Q&A sessions, and NGO showcases.
- Presentation competition themes: AI & digital innovation, sustainability, climate action, education, community health.
- **Abstract Submission Guidelines (display as a clearly formatted rulebook card):**
  - Each submitted paper must include: title of the paper, name(s) of author(s), affiliation(s), phone number, and email ID of the corresponding author.
  - The first author and presenting author(s) must be undergraduate students (BE/B.Tech / Dual Degree).
  - Other co-authors may include undergraduate, postgraduate, or PhD students, as well as faculty members.
  - No restriction on number of authors or number of abstract submissions per participant.
  - Each submission requires a **separate registration**, completed before the submission deadline.
  - Certificates awarded to all registered participants who attend the conference.
- Rewards: Trophies and goodies for theme-wise winners.
